Running a boiler for condensing

Instead of switching the boiler on and off with hot water, the ESP32 sets its water temperature for the whole heating season. The boiler runs long, at a low flame, and the water comes back cool, so it condenses.

Work out the water temperature

Move the sliders. This is how the ESP32 calculates the flow temperature. The starting values are meant to be tuned in the first week.

Curve settings (tuned from HA)

Formula: water = target + slope × (target − outdoor) + correction × (target − room), clamped to 20–60 °C. The return is an estimate; the boiler reports the real value over OpenTherm. Set the starting curve so that at +2…+7 °C outside it gives roughly what the boiler gives today, and only then lower it.

  • generic_thermostat stays as a safety cut-off with a wide hysteresis (about 1.5 °C), so it almost never interrupts the burner.

Weather curve + room correction

  • Calculates the water temperature from the outdoor and target temperatures.
  • Room too warm: cooler water, smaller flame. It does not turn the boiler off.
  • Central heating stays on for the whole season. It turns off only when the room overheats or it is warm outside (summer threshold).

When HA is down

The ESP does not reboot without HA. After about 10 minutes it keeps heating on the curve with the last target.

Fallback

Keep the old control (e.g. an ON/OFF relay) at hand. To go back: 2 wires back on the thermostat input and the old heater:.

Where the gas saving comes from

Cool return

< 45 °C

Below this temperature the water vapour in the flue gas condenses and gives off its heat. Goal: most of the heating time.

Fewer burner starts

starts / day ↓

Every start means a purge and running outside condensing. Count starts per day before the change so you have a baseline.

Gentle night setbacks

−1…1.5 °C

A deep setback means hot water in the morning and no condensing. Better a small setback and a limited morning boost.

Hydraulics at the boiler

to check

All radiators open, lower pump speed, anti-cycling parameter in the boiler menu.

If you read the gas meter by hand, day to day you see the effect in the boiler data. Once a month, compare gas use normalised for outdoor temperature (degree days) before and after the change.

Rollout order

  1. Test: diagnostics and scannerfirst step

    During the day, in mild weather, briefly. Write down the boiler settings first. Disconnect the old control from the thermostat input for the test.

  2. Target ESPHome config

    Curve, room correction, safeguards. Add the device in HA.

  3. generic_thermostat on the OpenTherm switch

    Wide hysteresis; back up the file before the change.

  4. "Burner today" counter

    history_stats based on the flame instead of relay on-time.

  5. Voice assistant

    Move the aliases from the old switch to the new one.

  6. Old control kept as a fallback
  7. First week of tuning

    Lower the curve step by step while the house stays warm. In frost, watch the coldest room.

  8. "Boiler" dashboard

    Flow and return, modulation, flame, starts, pressure, faults.
